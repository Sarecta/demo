Services

Everything the car needs, done in one visit.

Exterior wash and decontamination

pH neutral foam bath, two bucket hand wash, iron and tar removal, clay treatment, then a plush towel dry. Sealant on top.

Interior deep clean

Steam and hot water extraction for carpet and upholstery, leather cleaned and conditioned, vents, seams and glass.

Wheels, tires and calipers

Barrels cleaned front to back, brake dust dissolved, tires dressed to a satin finish rather than a shine.

Paint correction

Single or multi stage machine polishing. Swirls, wash marks, oxidation and light scratches cut back under inspection lighting, then refined for gloss.

Ceramic coating

Prepped and coated for years of protection instead of weeks. Water sheets off, dirt struggles to stick, and the paint keeps its depth. One to five year options.
